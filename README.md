This repository contains the full research paper titled **“Swara Yoga: A Historical, Philosophical, and Scientific Analysis.”**

🕉️ **Abstract**  
Swara Yoga is the ancient science of breath awareness, based on the flow of prana through the nostrils. Rooted in the Shiva Swarodaya and Advaita Vedanta, this paper explores the metaphysical and physiological insights of Swara Yoga and bridges it with modern neuroscience, brain hemisphere studies, and autonomic nervous system research.

📘 **Topics Covered**  
- Historical roots in Shiva Swarodaya  
- Philosophical foundation in Vedanta & Tantra  
- Nasal dominance and energy channels (Ida, Pingala, Sushumna)  
- Meditation, therapy, and daily decision-making  
- Neuroscience validation through modern studies
